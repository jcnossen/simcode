Fig 2 (\label{fig:simulation}), simulation at 1 emitter/µm²/frame, 60 000 frames, 40 x 40 px of 108.33 nm (4.33 µm field). Simulated with the in-situ vectorial PSF of the 1809/2 movie and that movie's sCMOS camera maps (gain, offset, per-pixel read noise); emitter heights 0-400 nm above the coverslip (median 116 nm); experimental photons and background of 1809/2 (median 621 detected photons per 6-frame window, background 1.95 photons/px/frame); mean on-time 20 frames; pattern 2 angles x 3 phase steps, pitch 224 nm, modulation depth 0.9.
(A) Ground truth: all labelling sites of the microtubules of webSMLM's cell model (the inSiliScope CellField world model; seed 11, 37427 labelling sites at 0.50 per nm of filament, 75 µm of microtubule in the field) (orange); white box = the zoom of B, C, F-H (1.73 µm, centred on filament crossings). Ground truth from webSMLM (microtubules (cells) sample, which embeds the inSiliScope world model), webSMLM defaults except the label efficiency (0.5 labels per nm of filament); the labels sit on the 13-protofilament lattice with a 12 nm binder and 2-5 nm linker (~25-30 nm from the tube axis), their heights are drawn from the experimental height distribution as for every emitter (see above).
(B) The ground truth of the zoom (all labelling sites), rendered exactly as the reconstructions. (C, F, G, H) Renders of the zoom by (C) SMLM, (F) SIMFLUX, (G) DECODE and (H) SIMCODE; same rendering for B, C, F-H (5.4 nm pixels, 1-pixel Gaussian blur, 99.5 % clip). Rendered = each method's comparison set: one localization per emitter and disjoint 6-frame window, estimated precision < 0.15 px; SIMCODE additionally p >= 0.63 and calibrated max(σx, σy) <= 12 nm. SIMFLUX uses the pattern estimated from the data, as SIMCODE does (true-pattern results: SI table). SMLM and DECODE get 6-frame sums; DECODE was retrained for this PSF, the emitter heights and these photon / background / density ranges. GT-FRC in the titles = resolution from the FRC between the rendered reconstruction and the rendered ground truth (5 nm pixels, 1/7 threshold, full field, 60 000 frames). Resolution = 1 / the spatial frequency at which the FRC (5-sample moving average) falls below 1/7 and stays below it (for at least 0.01 nm⁻¹; sustained crossing): the labels sit on a shell about 25-30 nm from the microtubule axis, so the ground-truth spectrum has a null near 1/(60-65 nm) where every curve dips and recovers; that dip is not taken as the resolution.
(D) the in-situ vectorial PSF fit the movies were simulated with (Zernike Noll 5-21), xy section at the emitters' median height (116 nm) and xz section, log scale (3 decades); 68 mλ rms aberration (Zernike modes 5-21). xz section: white dashed line = focal plane, cyan dotted line = median emitter height.
Cramér-Rao lower bounds for a single isolated emitter (mean over sub-pixel positions and pattern phases, 16 x 16 px ROI, z free) at 621 detected photons per 6-frame window, background 1.95 photons/px/frame and camera read noise 1.3 e- rms: SMLM (the 6-frame sum) x/y/z 12.7/12.7/58 nm, SIMFLUX (the 6 frames with the known pattern: pitch 224 nm, depth 0.9, 2 x 3 phase steps) x/y/z 5.0/5.0/50 nm.
(E) Number of localizations in the comparison set: SMLM 45291, SIMFLUX 18761, DECODE 74186, SIMCODE 109086; hatched = false positives (no emitter ON in the localization's window within 3 σ, σ = the localization's own precision): SMLM 41.4 %, SIMFLUX 20.9 %, DECODE 7.3 %, SIMCODE 2.5 %.
(I) FRC curves of each method's reconstruction against the ground truth (60 000 frames); dashed line = 1/7; resolution in the legend; curves smoothed with a 5-sample moving average (as for the resolution values), the first two frequency samples (edge of the smoothing) not drawn. Half-split FRC of the same sets: SI table.
(J) Time to fidelity: GT-FRC resolution (same crossing rule) on the first N frames (N = 250 ... 60 000); dotted line = 30 nm. Frames to 30 nm (log-interpolated between the N): SMLM not reached, SIMFLUX 31.9k, DECODE not reached, SIMCODE 8.2k. The per-density summary (0.5 / 1 / 2 / 4 emitters/µm²/frame: localizations, GT-FRC, frames to 30 nm, half-split FRC, 10 nm Jaccard, false-positive fraction, with and without the true pattern) is in the Supplementary Information.
