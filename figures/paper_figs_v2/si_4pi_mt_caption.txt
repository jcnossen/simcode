What the timeline measures: the half-split FRC is the agreement of two independent halves of the data. On DNA-PAINT data it is set by how densely the localizations sample the labelled structure (docking sites revisited in both halves) and by the localization precision; the halves are kept apart in blocks of up to 500 windows because short blocks put the repeated detections of one binding event into both halves, and the FRC then measures precision (on MT 12 nm after only 1000 frames). It is not a ground-truth resolution: it does not see structure that is missing or misplaced in both halves alike. A curve that still falls at the end of the series means that the later frames still added sampling.
