READING
1. Short blocks measure precision, not the image: with blocks shorter than the same-molecule correlation time (h: repeated detections of one binding event and rebinding stay correlated for ~100-300 windows on these data, much longer than in the simulation) the half-split FRC of the first 1000 frames is 12 nm on MT, better than with all 1.8 M frames. Only independent halves (>= 500-window blocks) give a usable timeline. 2. With independent halves the MT curves fall over the whole series (no plateau: within 10 % of the all-frames value only after ~1.1-1.4 M of 1.8 M frames), with a step where the curve passes the hollow-tube spectral dip; the NPC curves flatten after ~0.2 M of 0.68 M frames. 3. Frames from across the series agree less than consecutive frames (dotted vs solid; MT densest region 25 nm after 296k instead of 75k frames). Two causes, not separable here: adjacent 500-window blocks still share some correlated detections across their boundaries (h: at 300-1000 windows the MT ratio is 1.6 against 1.3 at long lags), and files far apart are less consistent with each other (per-file 4Pi registration and in-situ calibration, residual drift); there is no sign that the late frames themselves are worse (the spread curves end at the same value). The first-N curves are therefore somewhat optimistic for a shorter acquisition. 4. The half-split FRC is a much stricter and different quantity than the simulation's ground-truth FRC: in the simulation at 0.84x the densest real region (g) the ground-truth FRC reaches 25 nm after 11 000 frames and 18 nm at 30 000 frames, while the half-split FRC of the same localizations is still 81 nm (both halves must sample the same sites; the ground truth is complete). The simulation's 'faithful image after ~11 000 frames' therefore cannot be checked with the real data's half-split FRC, and the real timelines are not a test of it. 5. Structure completeness saturates much earlier than the FRC: the MT skeleton is 99 % covered (>= 3 4Pi-SIMCODE localizations within +-30 nm) after 204,201 frames (4Pi-SIMFLUX: 90 % after 544,463), and 4Pi-SIMCODE sees 95 % of the NPC corners after 26,594 frames (4Pi-SIMFLUX 95,816). 6. The NPC two-ring valley / peak of the 4Pi-SIMCODE rows is best at ~0.13-0.26 M frames and worsens with the later files (z consistency between files), so the NPC 3D result would not suffer from stopping at about a third of the series.
