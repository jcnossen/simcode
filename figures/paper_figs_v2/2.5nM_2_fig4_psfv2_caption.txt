Fig. 4. DNA-PAINT imaging of tubulin in COS-7 cells at medium emitter density (2.5 nM CF660 imager; recording 2.5nM/2): 180 000 frames = 30 000 cycles of 2 pattern orientations x 3 phase steps, 5 ms exposure per frame, 120 x 120 camera pixels of 108.33 nm. Every method localizes 6-frame windows (one pattern cycle); only windows that start at a cycle boundary are used, so no photon is counted twice, and all methods are compared on the same windows and the same region (the central 11.9 x 11.9 µm of the field). All methods: precision < 0.15 px (16.25 nm) on their own reported uncertainty: CRLB for SMLM / SIMFLUX, network uncertainty for DECODE, calibrated uncertainty for SIMCODE (the larger of the x and y values), after each method's own quality filters (k); SIMCODE keeps only detections with a detection probability of at least 0.63. Each method's sample drift is estimated from its own localizations (DME v2) and removed. SMLM and SIMFLUX: 2D fits at the fit plane that gave each baseline its best FRC (0.318 µm for SMLM, 0.086 µm for SIMFLUX; tested the median network height 0.086 µm and the in-situ calibration's spot-fit median 0.318 µm).
Renders: every localization drawn as a Gaussian spot of 22 nm sd (a) or 5.4 nm sd (zooms) on 10.8 nm (2.7 nm) pixels, colour scale saturated at the 99th percentile of each image's pixels. Titles: the method and its FRC resolution (j).
(a) SIMCODE, full field. Solid and dashed boxes: the two zoom regions; they are the regions of the first version of this figure, kept unchanged (not re-picked for the new results).
(b-e) Zoom 1 (3.25 x 3.25 µm, solid box) by SMLM, SIMFLUX, DECODE and SIMCODE.
(f-i) Zoom 2 (1.62 x 1.62 µm, dashed box), same order.
(j) Fourier ring correlation (FRC) between two halves of each method's localizations (alternating blocks of 300 frames), rendered on 5 nm pixels; curves smoothed over 5 frequency bins. Resolution (legend) = the frequency where the curve first falls below 1/7 (dashed line): SMLM 48.5 nm, SIMFLUX 34.0 nm, DECODE 42.1 nm, SIMCODE 23.8 nm.
(k) Localizations per method: accepted (solid; the localizations of the renders and the FRC) and removed by the method's quality filters (light, hatched), counted on the same windows and region; the percentage above each bar is accepted / all localizations the method reported (SMLM and SIMFLUX: every detected spot with an SMLM fit at the method's own fit plane, so the two counts differ; DECODE: every localization it reported; SIMCODE: every detection with a detection probability of at least 0.63). Accepted: SMLM 0.81 million (33 %), SIMFLUX 0.13 million (6 %), DECODE 1.53 million (14 %), SIMCODE 5.37 million (81 %). Quality filters: SMLM: fits that fail the goodness-of-fit test (chi-square above 4 times the number of fitted pixels) or have a precision of 0.15 px or worse; SIMFLUX: the same goodness-of-fit test on the SMLM fit, a pattern-modulation error of 0.1 or more, a SIMFLUX fit that ends at a parameter bound or more than 0.5 px from its SMLM position, or a precision of 0.15 px or worse; DECODE: a network uncertainty of 0.15 px or worse; SIMCODE: a calibrated uncertainty of 0.15 px or worse.
(l) Resolution-scaled Pearson correlation (RSP) of each method's render with the widefield image (the mean of all frames): SMLM 0.63, SIMFLUX 0.22, DECODE 0.62, SIMCODE 0.71.
(m) The in-situ PSF of this recording (vectorial model fitted to the data, Zernike modes 5-21; 72 mλ rms aberration): xy section at the median emitter height (106 nm above the coverslip) and xz section, log scale over 3 decades; in the xz section the white dashed line marks the focal plane and the cyan dotted line the median emitter height. Scale bars 500 nm. Cramér-Rao lower bounds for one isolated emitter at the median signal of the SIMCODE localizations (862 detected photons per 6-frame window), median background 3.48 photons per pixel and frame, camera read noise 1.4 e- rms and the median emitter height: SMLM (sum of the 6 frames) x / y / z 11.1 / 11.9 / 59 nm; SIMFLUX (the 6 frames with this recording's estimated pattern: pitch 224 nm, modulation depth 0.78 / 0.80 (orientations 1 / 2), 2 orientations x 3 phase steps) x / y / z 4.8 / 5.0 / 49 nm.
