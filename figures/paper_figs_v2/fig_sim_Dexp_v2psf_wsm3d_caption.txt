Fig. 2. Simulation at 1 emitter per µm² per frame, 60 000 frames, 40 x 40 camera pixels of 108.33 nm (4.33 µm field). Simulated with the in-situ vectorial PSF of the recording of Fig. 3 and that recording's sCMOS camera maps (gain, offset, per-pixel read noise); photons and background as measured in that recording (median 621 detected photons per 6-frame window, background 1.95 photons per pixel and frame); mean on-time 20 frames; pattern of 2 orientations x 3 phase steps, pitch 224 nm, modulation depth 0.9.
(a) Ground truth: all labelling sites of a simulated microtubule network (webSMLM, microtubules-in-cells sample; 0.5 labels per nm of filament, 75 µm of filament, 37 427 sites in the field), colour = height above the coverslip (0-250 nm colour scale; brightness = label density). The labels sit ~25-30 nm from the tube axis (13-protofilament lattice, 12 nm binder, 2-5 nm linker). Every label is simulated at its own 3D position on the filament (heights 34-225 nm above the coverslip, 5th-95th percentile, median 135 nm; the 1.6 % of label positions below the coverslip surface are set to 0 nm). White box: the zoom of b-f (1.73 x 1.73 µm, picked at filament crossings). (b) The ground truth of the zoom, rendered as the reconstructions (colour = height, brightness as in c-f).
(c-f) Renders of the zoom by (c) SMLM, (d) SIMFLUX, (e) DECODE and (f) SIMCODE (5.4 nm pixels, 1-pixel Gaussian blur, colour scale saturated at the 99th percentile of the image's pixels). One selection rule for every method, the rule used for the experimental data, set before the analysis and without the ground truth: each method's localizations on disjoint 6-frame windows (one localization per window and emitter, no photon used twice) that pass the method's own quality filters and have a reported precision below 0.15 px (16.25 nm; the larger of the x and y values; SMLM / SIMFLUX: CRLB; DECODE: its network uncertainty; SIMCODE: the posterior uncertainty of its pattern solve, with its detection probability of at least 0.63). Titles: GT-FRC resolution and the number of localizations. SIMFLUX uses the pattern estimated from the data, as SIMCODE does. SMLM and DECODE get the sum of the 6 frames; SMLM and SIMFLUX fit a 2D model of the PSF at the median emitter height (135 nm); DECODE was retrained for this PSF following its published training settings (Supplementary Note: DECODE training), with emitter heights of 0-400 nm and these photon, background and density ranges. GT-FRC = the FRC between the rendered reconstruction and the rendered ground truth (x-y projection, 5 nm pixels, full field, all 60 000 frames); resolution = the frequency after which the curve stays below 1/7 for 0.01 nm^-1 (the hollow tube's spectrum has zeros near 65, 28 and 18 nm, where a curve can dip below 1/7 and recover).
(g) The rendered localizations by their 2D error to the nearest emitter that is on in the window: <= 20 nm (solid), 20-50 nm (light), no emitter on within 50 nm = false positive (hatched): SMLM 48 439 (21 724 / 17 022 / 9 693); SIMFLUX 22 223 (21 146 / 1 048 / 29); DECODE 124 048 (99 399 / 22 806 / 1 843); SIMCODE 138 608 (127 528 / 10 586 / 494). Number above each bar: rms error per axis of the true positives (localizations with an emitter on within 3 times their own reported precision): SMLM 12.9 nm, SIMFLUX 4.8 nm, DECODE 10.1 nm, SIMCODE 7.0 nm.
(h) GT-FRC curves (all 60 000 frames); dashed line = 1/7; resolution in the legend. The curves are drawn exactly as the resolution is read from them: smoothed over 5 frequency bins (a 5-bin moving average, no extra smoothing for the plot); the first two frequency bins are not drawn.
(i) Time to fidelity: GT-FRC of the same selection on the first N frames (N = 250 ... 60 000); dotted line = 30 nm. Frames to reach 40 / 30 / 20 nm: SMLM 55.9k / not reached / not reached; SIMFLUX 8.7k / 16.8k / 57.2k; DECODE 5.1k / 21.8k / not reached; SIMCODE 1.8k / 4.9k / 16.3k.
(j) The PSF of the simulation (the in-situ vectorial PSF fit of the recording of Fig. 3, Zernike modes 5-21; 68 mλ rms aberration): xy section at the median emitter height (135 nm) and xz section, log scale over 3 decades; in the xz section the white dashed line marks the focal plane and the cyan dotted line the median emitter height. Scale bars 500 nm.
