Fig. 2. Simulation at 1 emitter per µm² per frame, 60 000 frames, 40 x 40 camera pixels of 108.33 nm (4.33 µm field). Simulated with the in-situ vectorial PSF of the recording of Fig. 3 and that recording's sCMOS camera maps (gain, offset, per-pixel read noise); emitter heights 0-400 nm above the coverslip (median 116 nm); photons and background as measured in that recording (median 621 detected photons per 6-frame window, background 1.95 photons per pixel and frame); mean on-time 20 frames; pattern of 2 orientations x 3 phase steps, pitch 224 nm, modulation depth 0.9.
(a) Ground truth: all labelling sites (orange) of a simulated microtubule network (webSMLM, microtubules-in-cells sample; 0.5 labels per nm of filament, 75 µm of filament, 37 427 sites in the field). The labels sit ~25-30 nm from the tube axis (13-protofilament lattice, 12 nm binder, 2-5 nm linker); their heights are drawn from the experimental height distribution. White box: the zoom of b, c and f-h (1.73 x 1.73 µm, picked at filament crossings). (b) The ground truth of the zoom, rendered as the reconstructions.
(c, f-h) Renders of the zoom by (c) SMLM, (f) SIMFLUX, (g) DECODE and (h) SIMCODE (5.4 nm pixels, 1-pixel Gaussian blur, colour scale saturated at the 99th percentile of the image's pixels). Each method's localizations on disjoint 6-frame windows (one localization per window and emitter, no photon used twice) with a precision < 0.15 px (16.2 nm; the larger of the x and y values; SIMCODE also needs a detection probability of at least 0.63), then cut at the threshold on the method's own reported precision (SMLM / SIMFLUX: CRLB; DECODE: its network uncertainty; SIMCODE: its calibrated uncertainty) that gives the best GT-FRC: SMLM 16.2 nm, SIMFLUX 12.2 nm, DECODE 11.7 nm, SIMCODE 10.2 nm. Titles: GT-FRC resolution and the localizations kept (% of the localizations before the cut). SIMFLUX uses the pattern estimated from the data, as SIMCODE does. SMLM and DECODE get the sum of the 6 frames; DECODE was retrained for this PSF, the emitter heights and these photon, background and density ranges. GT-FRC = the FRC between the rendered reconstruction and the rendered ground truth (5 nm pixels, full field, all 60 000 frames); resolution = the frequency after which the curve stays below 1/7 for 0.01 nm^-1 (the hollow tube's spectrum has zeros near 65, 28 and 18 nm, where a curve can dip below 1/7 and recover).
(d) GT-FRC vs the fraction of localizations kept when the precision threshold is lowered from all localizations to 10 %; dot = the chosen threshold. Choosing the threshold by the FRC between two halves of the data instead (no ground truth, as for the experimental data) changes the GT-FRC by SMLM +3.2 nm, SIMFLUX +0.0 nm, DECODE +0.9 nm, SIMCODE +0.0 nm.
(e) The rendered localizations by their 2D error to the nearest emitter that is on in the window: <= 20 nm (solid), 20-50 nm (light), no emitter on within 50 nm = false positive (hatched): SMLM 45 291 (17 192 / 17 010 / 11 089); SIMFLUX 18 761 (17 517 / 1 197 / 47); DECODE 37 093 (30 748 / 5 863 / 482); SIMCODE 82 591 (79 467 / 3 094 / 30). Number above each bar: rms error per axis of the true positives (localizations with an emitter on within 3 times their own reported precision): SMLM 13.9 nm, SIMFLUX 5.3 nm, DECODE 9.0 nm, SIMCODE 6.5 nm.
(i) GT-FRC curves of the rendered sets (all 60 000 frames); dashed line = 1/7; resolution in the legend. The curves are drawn exactly as the resolution is read from them: smoothed over 5 frequency bins (a 5-bin moving average, no extra smoothing for the plot); the first two frequency bins are not drawn.
(j) Time to fidelity: GT-FRC of the same selection on the first N frames (N = 250 ... 60 000); dotted line = 30 nm. Frames to reach 40 / 30 / 20 nm: SMLM none reached; SIMFLUX 10.0k / 31.9k / not reached; DECODE 18.9k / not reached / not reached; SIMCODE 2.5k / 8.4k / 33.6k.
(k) The PSF of the simulation (the in-situ vectorial PSF fit of the recording of Fig. 3, Zernike modes 5-21; 68 mλ rms aberration): xy section at the median emitter height (116 nm) and xz section, log scale over 3 decades; in the xz section the white dashed line marks the focal plane and the cyan dotted line the median emitter height. Scale bars 500 nm. Cramér-Rao lower bounds for a single isolated emitter (mean over sub-pixel positions and pattern phases, 16 x 16 px ROI, z free) at 621 detected photons per 6-frame window, background 1.95 photons/px/frame and camera read noise 1.3 e- rms: SMLM (the 6-frame sum) x/y/z 12.7/12.7/58 nm, SIMFLUX (the 6 frames with the known pattern: pitch 224 nm, depth 0.9, 2 x 3 phase steps) x/y/z 5.0/5.0/50 nm.
