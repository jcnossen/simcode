--- caption text ---

Comparison of the estimation precision of conventional MLE fitters (SMLM, SIMFLUX) with our deep-learning model at constant background (A) and
under fluctuating background (B, C), where each frame has a background drawn from U(1, 6) photons/px. Isolated emitters in 6 x 16 x 16 px regions
(108.33 nm pixels; the regions sit in a 4 x 4 grid in 80 x 80 px fields so that the network sees image context), each emitter uniformly 5-11 px inside its
region; the in-situ vectorial PSF fitted on the 1809/2 data set, all emitters at that data set's median emitter height (116 nm); modulation (2 orientations
x 3 phase steps, pitch 224 nm, depth 0.9) and pixel size as our experimental setup; pure Poisson noise. The x axis is the total number of detected signal
photons over the 6 frames (photons collected by the objective for an emitter at 116 nm above the coverslip). Each bin has 1008 emitters; the precision is the RMSD over all fitted (SMLM, SIMFLUX) or detected (NN Output, SIMCODE) emitters, without outlier trimming.
(A) Constant background of 1 photon/px/frame. The SMLM fit starts from the centroid of the 6-frame sum (background = ROI-border median) and the SIMFLUX fit from the SMLM fit, as in the experimental pipeline; at 100 photons 6.6 % of the SIMFLUX fits converge one fringe off (|error| > 112 nm), 0.1 % at 147 photons, none from 215 photons up. (B) Fluctuating background. NN Output is the deep-learning model's output prior to fitting with the
modulation pattern. SIMCODE is the final estimate (pattern-aware networks followed by the explicit fit with the modulation pattern; its uncertainty is the fit's posterior standard deviation). Dashed lines: CRLB
computed from the ground truth; the 'SIMCODE bound' is the SIMFLUX CRLB with a separate background per frame (the model SIMCODE fits; no separate
SIMCODE CRLB is computed). Dotted lines (B): predicted precision, i.e. the mean of the method's own uncertainty estimate. Open symbols: bins in which the network
detected fewer than 50 % of the emitters (only the 100-photon bin: 26 % at constant, 8.5 % at fluctuating background); the error there is over the
detected emitters. (C) One example 6-frame stack at 1468 photons under fluctuating background, with each frame's background (photons/px) and the true
position (red cross).
