--- caption text ---

Figure S10. Localization precision versus the Cramér-Rao lower bound.

Isolated emitters in 6-frame stacks: 16 x 16 px regions (108.33 nm pixels), placed as a 4 x 4 grid in 80 x 80 px fields (the networks need
image context beyond a 16 px region), each emitter uniformly 5-11 px inside its region. PSF: the in-situ vectorial PSF fitted on the 1809/2 data set,
with every emitter at the median emitter height of that data set (116 nm). Illumination: two pattern orientations x three phase steps, pitch 224 nm,
modulation depth 0.9 (random phase offset per orientation and field). Pure Poisson noise. 1008 emitters per photon bin; the x axis is the number of
detected signal photons over the 6 frames (100-3162, 10 log-spaced bins).
(A) Constant background of 1 photon/px/frame. (B, C) Fluctuating background: every frame has its own background drawn from U(1, 6) photons/px.
Symbols and solid lines: root-mean-square error in x of SMLM (MLE on the 6-frame sum with the same PSF), SIMFLUX (joint MLE of the 6 frames with the
known pattern), the network output before the pattern fit (NN output: the pattern-free position estimate of the pass-1 network) and SIMCODE
(final output: pattern-aware networks + explicit pattern fit, given the true pattern). In C SIMFLUX is shown with the model of the original pipeline
(one background for all six frames) and with a background per frame. The 2 % largest errors per bin and method are removed. Dashed lines: CRLB
computed from the ground truth, in the colour of the method it bounds (B: the SMLM CRLB; C: the SIMFLUX CRLB with a background per frame). Dotted lines:
predicted precision, i.e. the mean of the method's own reported uncertainty. Open symbols: bins in which the network detected fewer than 50 % of the
emitters (100 photons: 26 % at constant, 8.5 % at fluctuating background); the error there is over the detected emitters.

Result (numbers for the text):
- From 316 photons up, SIMCODE reaches 1.01-1.29 x the SIMFLUX CRLB at constant background (1.01-1.10 up to 1000 photons, rising to 1.29 at 3162)
  and 1.11-1.26 x the per-frame-background SIMFLUX CRLB under fluctuating background.
- The NN output follows the SMLM CRLB (e.g. 14.4 vs 15.0 nm at 316 photons, 3.50 vs 3.47 nm at 3162 photons, constant background).
- Under fluctuating background the one-background SIMFLUX model fails (39.1 nm at 316 photons against a bound of 8.5 nm), while the
  per-frame-background SIMFLUX reaches its bound (8.2 nm).
- SIMCODE's reported uncertainty is conservative on these idealised isolated emitters: 1.33-1.58 x the actual error at constant background and
  1.16-1.53 x under fluctuating background, from 316 photons up (on the realistic simulations it is calibrated).
- Detection: >= 97 % of the emitters from 147 photons up at constant background, >= 96 % from 215 photons up at fluctuating background
  (old PSF / old networks: 51 % / 15 % at 147 photons).

Old vs new (same protocol; old = averaged in-situ PSF v1 at 55 nm, x axis = the PSF model's intensity parameter):
- SIMCODE / SIMFLUX CRLB from 316 photons up: constant 1.02-1.19 -> 1.01-1.29; fluctuating 1.09-1.25 -> 1.11-1.26.
- Predicted / actual: constant 1.50-1.61 -> 1.33-1.58; fluctuating 1.17-1.49 -> 1.16-1.53.
- One-background SIMFLUX at 316 photons, fluctuating: 46 nm vs bound 10.4 nm -> 39.1 nm vs 8.5 nm.
- Absolute values are not comparable bin by bin: the x axis changed from the intensity parameter to detected photons (the PSF v2 image at 116 nm holds
  0.722 of the intensity parameter), and the PSF / height changed (SIMFLUX CRLB at 316 photons, constant bg: 7.12 -> 6.01 nm).

  preprint protocol; the v2 camera's read noise is NOT included). SMLM / SIMFLUX fits and CRLBs: zpsf.load(PSF v2), 2D fits at the known height
