Simulated moving microtubules: the preprint's simulation (15 microtubules with Brownian motion and spring-like mechanics, 50 x 50 px of 108.33 nm, 64000 frames after 1000 skipped), rendered with the in-situ PSF of 1809/2 and the sCMOS camera maps of that movie (gain, offset, per-pixel read noise), emitter heights 0-400 nm (median 118 nm, one height per labelling site); experimental photons and background of 1809/2 (median 621 detected photons per 6-frame window, lognormal, 1.94 photons/px/frame, mean on-time 20 frames); 2 x 3-step pattern, pitch 224 nm, depth 0.9. The reconstruction is divided into 64 blocks of 1000 frames; each block gives one reconstructed frame (movie in the supplemental media).

(A) One input frame and the reconstructions of two blocks (frames 10000-11000 and 40000-41000); ground truth = all positions of the labelling sites during the block. Every method is shown with the localizations that enter its FRC (disjoint 6-frame windows, σ < 0.15 px; SIMCODE v2: localizations with calibrated precision ≤ 12 nm); each tile gives the block's FRC and number of localizations. SIMFLUX and the preprint SIMCODE use the pattern estimated from the data, as SIMCODE v2 does; DECODE was retrained for this PSF, the emitter heights and the photon / background / density ranges.

(B): the in-situ vectorial PSF fit the movies were simulated with (Zernike Noll 5-21), xy section at the emitters' median height (118 nm) and xz section, log scale (3 decades); rms aberration over Noll >= 5 68 mλ. xz section: white dashed line = focal plane, cyan dotted line = median emitter height.
Cramér-Rao lower bounds for a single isolated emitter (mean over sub-pixel positions and pattern phases, 16 x 16 px ROI, z free) at 621 detected photons per 6-frame window, background 1.94 photons/px/frame and camera read noise 1.3 e- rms: SMLM (the 6-frame sum) x/y/z 12.7/12.6/58 nm, SIMFLUX (the 6 frames with the known pattern: pitch 224 nm, depth 0.9, 2 x 3 phase steps) x/y/z 5.0/5.0/50 nm.

(C) FRC of each 1000-frame block (half-split within the block, halves alternating every 60 frames). (D) Localization error against the ground truth: each localization is matched to the nearest emitter that is on in its 6-frame window (photon-weighted mean position over the window) within 100 nm; bars = RMS error of the matched localizations, diamonds = % of localizations that are unmatched or more than 30 nm off. (E) Mean Jaccard index over the blocks at 10 / 20 / 40 nm (the preprint's measure: ground truth = all trajectory points of the block). With ~10^6 ground-truth points per block it mostly measures how much of the moving structure is covered, so it grows with the number of localizations rather than with their accuracy; read it together with C and D.
