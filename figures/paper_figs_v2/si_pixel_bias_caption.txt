Pixel bias of the localization methods. (a) Sub-pixel histograms (x solid, y dashed; position within the pixel, 0 = pixel centre; 20 bins, normalised to 1 for a uniform histogram) on the D1 simulation, with the ground-truth positions and, for DECODE's published model, its own simulated conditions (10 emitters per 40 x 40 px frame). |c1| = amplitude of the first Fourier harmonic of the histogram (x / y; 0 for a uniform histogram, noise floor 1/sqrt(n)). (b) Bias toward the pixel centre (first-harmonic amplitude A of the error vs the true sub-pixel position, e = -A sin(2 pi f); filled x, open y; +-1 SE) of isolated emitters vs detected photons per 6-frame window (each method's own filters, without the common precision cut, which removes most dim SMLM and DECODE localizations). (c) The same for DECODE's published model in its own conditions (astigmatic PSF, EMCCD, 127 x 117 nm pixels, photons per frame). (d, e) Bias and |c1| vs density (emitters ON per um^2 per window), DECODE's published model at 10 and 50 emitters per frame.
Rows: the common rule for every method (own filters, disjoint 6-frame windows, max(sigma_x, sigma_y) < 0.15 px on its own reported uncertainty; SIMCODE p >= 0.63 with calibrated sigma). Bias: rows matched within 1 px to a ground-truth emitter with no other emitter ON within 2 px. SIMFLUX uses the true pattern throughout; on the isolated-emitter movie SIMCODE also uses the true pattern (its pattern estimate fails on this sparse movie).
DECODE training: DECODE was retrained on our conditions following the training settings published with DECODE (AdamW, learning rate 6e-4, weight decay 0.1, ReduceLROnPlateau with factor 0.5, patience 20 and cooldown 10, batch 64, 32 768 simulated frames per epoch), from scratch with a 512-step linear learning-rate warm-up (without it the training diverged in the first epoch). It was trained to convergence (all monitored metrics flat from epoch ~200) and the weights were frozen at epoch 300 (9.8 M training frames). The training simulations use the in-situ PSF v2 model only (the PSF of these simulations). With this training DECODE's pixel bias is reduced (e.g. D1, all localizations: |c1| 0.30 / 0.25 -> 0.12 / 0.14 and bias 6.7 / 4.3 -> -0.2 / 1.8 nm x / y, vs the earlier, shorter-trained model) but does not go away.
