Intensities are normalized per localization by the sum of the 6 intensities of its window, mu_N_ij / sum_ij mu_N_ij, and plotted against the pattern phase q_i . Both methods are classified with the same rule, the SIMFLUX input filter: a localization is included (blue) if its modulation error gamma = max_ij |mu_N_ij / sum mu_N - r_ij (1 + m_i sin(phase_ij))| is below 0.1, and excluded (red) otherwise; each method uses its own intensities, its own pattern-free position and its own estimated pattern. SIMFLUX: per-frame intensities of the SMLM ROI fits and the SMLM position; population = the SMLM fits accepted by the baseline (chi^2 <= 4 x ROI^2, 8 x 8 px ROI). SIMCODE: pass-1 network intensities and the pass-1 network position (before the pattern is used); population = the detections with score p >= 0.63 (no precision cut). Both inside the field of view x, y in [5, 115) px, and both with at least 300 detected photons summed over the 6 frames of the method's own per-frame intensities (the photon threshold of the pattern estimation; both in photo-electrons with the same v2 camera gain and offset map), and isolated: no other detection of either method within 325 nm (3 px) in the same window. The neighbour list of a window is the union of both methods' detections: the stored SMLM fits of the 6-frame sum starting at that frame and the stored SIMCODE pass-1 candidates (score p >= 0.3) of the window starting at the same frame (SMLM windows starting off SIMCODE's 3-frame grid: the SIMCODE window with the nearest start); the localization itself and its counterpart in the other method (the nearest detection of the other method within 1 px, the same emitter) are not counted. 2000 random localizations per panel, in their true proportions (red drawn first, blue on top); the panel text gives the fraction within 0.1 of the whole population. Black: the estimated illumination pattern of each method (SIMFLUX: depth fixed to 0.9, as in the SIMFLUX pipeline; SIMCODE: the estimated depth), r_ij (1 + m_i sin(phase)).
