Simulated randomly generated microtubules, 60 000 frames, the in-situ PSF of 1809/2 and the sCMOS camera maps of that movie (gain, offset, per-pixel read noise), emitter heights 0-400 nm (median 116 nm); experimental photons and background of 1809/2 (median 621 detected photons per 6-frame window, 1.95 photons/px/frame); the same 2 x 3-step pattern for all densities.
(A) Renders of the same 1.73 µm crop for every method and emitter density (emitters/µm²/frame), same rendering in every tile; each tile gives the GT-FRC (FRC between the rendered reconstruction and the rendered ground truth of all labelling sites; 5 nm pixels, 1/7) and the number of localizations (disjoint 6-frame windows, σ < 0.15 px). SIMFLUX uses the pattern estimated from the data, as SIMCODE does; DECODE was retrained for this PSF, the emitter heights and these photon / background / density ranges. Results with the true pattern, the fair half-split FRC, the Jaccard index and the false-positive fraction are in the SI table. Resolution = 1 / the spatial frequency at which the FRC (5-sample moving average) falls below 1/7 and stays below it (for at least 0.01 nm⁻¹; sustained crossing): the labels sit on a shell about 25-30 nm from the microtubule axis, so the ground-truth spectrum has a null near 1/(60-65 nm) where every curve dips and recovers; that dip is not taken as the resolution.
