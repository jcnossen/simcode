Simulated microtubules of webSMLM's cell model (the inSiliScope CellField world model; seed 11, 37427 labelling sites at 0.50 per nm of filament, 75 µm of microtubule in the field), 60 000 frames, the in-situ PSF of 1809/2 and the sCMOS camera maps of that movie (gain, offset, per-pixel read noise), emitter heights = the labels' own 3D positions on the filaments (34-225 nm, 5th-95th percentile, median 135 nm); 500 detected photons per 6-frame window, background 2.5 photons/px/frame; the same 2 x 3-step pattern for all densities.
(A) Renders of the same 1.73 µm crop for every method and emitter density (emitters/µm²/frame), same rendering in every tile; each tile gives the GT-FRC (FRC between the rendered reconstruction and the rendered ground truth of all labelling sites; 5 nm pixels, 1/7) and the number of localizations (disjoint 6-frame windows, σ < 0.15 px; SIMCODE: detection probability >= 0.63 and its posterior σ (pattern solve) < 0.15 px, the same precision cut as every method). SIMFLUX uses the pattern estimated from the data, as SIMCODE does; DECODE was retrained for this PSF following its published training settings (Supplementary Note: DECODE training), with the emitter heights and these photon / background / density ranges. Results with the true pattern, the fair half-split FRC, the Jaccard index and the false-positive fraction are in the SI table. Resolution = 1 / the spatial frequency at which the FRC (5-sample moving average) falls below 1/7 and stays below it (for at least 0.01 nm⁻¹; sustained crossing): the labels sit on a shell about 25-30 nm from the microtubule axis, so the ground-truth spectrum has a null near 1/(60-65 nm) where every curve dips and recovers; that dip is not taken as the resolution.
(B): the in-situ vectorial PSF fit the movies were simulated with (Zernike Noll 5-21), xy section at the emitters' median height (135 nm) and xz section, log scale (3 decades); 68 mλ rms aberration (Zernike modes 5-21). xz section: white dashed line = focal plane, cyan dotted line = median emitter height.
